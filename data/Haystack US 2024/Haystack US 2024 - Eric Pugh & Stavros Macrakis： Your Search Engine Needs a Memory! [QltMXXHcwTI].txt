In databases, you write your SQL query, and you know exactly what should come out. It's well-defined; there's no question of whether it's a good result or a bad result—it may not be the result you wanted, but the result for the SQL query is well-defined. Another reason you want data is to adapt your results to the corpus, the collection of documents you have, and the user population. Each application of search has a different notion of relevance.
