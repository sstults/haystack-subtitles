**Audience Member (Q&A Session):**

What advice would you give to someone new to the industry about where they should focus their learning in search and AI?

**Charlie Hull:**

Great question! My advice would be to start by understanding search fundamentals: how search engines work, indexing, and retrieval processes. Focus on learning the tools of the trade, such as Elasticsearch or Solr, and understand their strengths and tradeoffs. It's also crucial to get hands-on experience, work on projects or join hackathons like our Search Hack Day. Keep abreast of AI's role in search, as AI-powered search continues to grow in importance. Lastly, connect with the community through platforms like Relevance Slack, where you can learn from experienced professionals and stay updated on developments in the field.
